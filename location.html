Driving & Parking in Carmel

Carmel-by-the-Sea does not use street numbers. You will find our wooden noren curtain on 6th Avenue between Mission and San Carlos Streets. Free street parking is available along 6th Ave and Ocean Avenue (please observe the 2-hour village signs during daylight hours).

Private Dining & Inquiries

For intimate gatherings, full 20-seat sanctuary buyouts, or chef-curated private omakase dinners, please telephone the restaurant directly at (831) 620-1516 during afternoon prep hours (2:00 PM – 4:30 PM).
