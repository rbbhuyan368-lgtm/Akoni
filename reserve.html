Seating & Grace Period

Due to our intimate 20-seat capacity, tables are reserved for 90 minutes. We hold reservations for up to 15 minutes past the scheduled seating time before releasing to walk-in guests.

Dietary Requests

We take pride in honoring traditional Edomae sushi. Please inform us of any shellfish, soy, or gluten sensitivities when booking so the chef can curate your pieces thoughtfully.
